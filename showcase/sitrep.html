Forever lets an addon know how far away an enemy is, but not where. The envelope shows each enemy's distance bracket against the zone its class can hit you from (red), your own spell ranges (green lines) and how fast it is closing. 1-1

What the client lets an addon see

Forever's client (build 1.60.1) is the Retail client with Midnight's secret-value rules. A secret value throws if addon code compares it, branches on it or does arithmetic with it. Most PvP addon ideas die here. The table below shows what testers measured in the beta and what SITREP does with each feed.

The loop

SITREP runs Boyd's OODA loop five times a second. Each stage feeds the next, and a fault in one stage is contained: a failing handler is reported once and the tick carries on. That matters because the client stops reporting Lua errors after 100 per session.

The engagement calculus

In aimed-fire combat every fighter can shoot at every enemy, so fighting strength grows with the square of numbers. Three against two is not 1.5 to 1. It is 2.25 to 1. That is Lanchester's square law, and group PvP is close to it: everyone can target anyone in range.

strength = N × Σ quality   ·   ρ = Sfriendly / Shostile   ·   P(win) = σ(k · ln ρ + b)

Quality combines class power, level difference, health, and the matchup matrix averaged over your side. Sightings count less as they age. If you win, expected losses are N × (1 − √(1 − 1/ρ)). The curve's k and b start at doctrine values and move with every fight you win or lose.

Player and target frames

A unit frame should answer the next decision in under a third of a second. SITREP's frames spend their space on two questions. Your frame asks whether you'll survive the next three seconds, and who is on you. Your target's frame asks whether you can hit it, whether you should, and who else is on it.

Both run on the simulation above and follow its top threat as your target. Switch "You play" to a healer and your own frame shows the heal you are casting. Hover a line in the anatomy to find it on the frame.

Pets and party

Pets, your target's pet and your party use the same frame language: health the client draws, a cast bar, and the same highlights. A red border means that member is being hit right now. A border in a debuff's colour means you can dispel it, so the frame you need to click lights up before you read a name.

The distance on each party row is exact, from positions the group shares over the addon channel. It turns red past your heal range. Switch "You play" above: as a Druid or Shaman the poisoned rogue lights up, as a Mage the cursed druid does.

7-1

Trackers

A tracker is one of your spells as an icon, placed anywhere on screen. It shows one condition and nothing else, so a glance answers one question: is my shield up, is my DoT on, is my interrupt ready. Each icon has its own position, size, opacity and an importance border in red, orange or green.

ON COOLDOWN

Shows while the spell is on cooldown, with a swipe.

INVERTED: NOT ON COOLDOWN, i.e. ready

ON TARGET

Shows while your aura is on your target, with its time left.

INVERTED: NOT ON TARGET, i.e. reapply

ON ME

Shows while the buff is on you. The buff can be named separately when the spell's buff has another name.

INVERTED: NOT ON ME, i.e. rebuff

ON WEAPON

Shows while a weapon imbue is on, main or off hand: Flametongue, Windfury, poisons, oils.

INVERTED: NOT ON WEAPON, i.e. reimbue

The spell field searches a library built from your spellbook, the auras on you and your target, every aura SITREP has seen, and spell IDs. Results scroll, and hovering one shows its full tooltip. The tracker list can be exported as a string and mirrored into macros, which survive a client that forgets addon settings.

8-1

Field use

Install

Copy the SITREP folder into the client's AddOns folder. The beta's product folder is _classic_beta_; the launch folder may differ.

World of Warcraft/_classic_beta_/Interface/AddOns/SITREP

Bind Broadcast contact report under Key Bindings → AddOns, then type /sitrep to open the console. Unlock the HUD there and drag the modules into place: envelope, threat board, engagement bar, pressure gauge, allies and every unit frame.

Duels

An attackable player of your own faction is a duel opponent, since you can only duel your own faction. Nameplates are re-sorted into friend and foe on every scan, so a duel partner leaves your side the moment the countdown ends. The result comes from the game's own "has defeated … in a duel" message. Duels are tagged DUEL in the Intel Log, train only the duel curve, and build your win rate against each class.

Reports

The client's chat can't be copied, so SITREP writes its diagnostics into its saved settings, where they can be read after /reload. After every fight it saves a report with a timeline of each tracker's source and how many "who targets whom" checks the client answered.

/sitrep auraprobe   what each aura source answers
/sitrep buffs       buff bar and tracker state
/sitrep weapon      weapon imbue sources
/sitrep tot         target's-target health feed

Verified headless

luajit tests/run.lua
684 passed, 0 failed  (LuaJIT 2.1 and Lua 5.5)

A client emulator drives scripted fights through every module. Its secret values throw on comparison, as the real ones do, and its buff bar turns secret on redraw, as the real one does. Source guards fail the build on the patterns that crashed in game: a boolean test on a secret cast flag, or a pcall whose second result gets dropped. A scan against the 6,045 global functions and 269 namespaces harvested from the Forever beta found no call to a missing API.

Limits

  1. The emulator reproduces the measured rules, not every quirk. Features reach the live client one test session at a time.
  2. No addon can show where an enemy stands on Forever: the client blocks nameplate positions and offers no coordinates. SITREP shows distance and closure instead.
  3. Enemy players may hide more identity than mobs. Contacts then fall back to GUID and "Unknown".
  4. SavedVariables are written on /reload and logout, but a cold start may not load them (a beta bug). Tracker lists can be mirrored into macros, and Intel Log → Export profile is the backup.
  5. In combat, a tracker watching an aura works from evidence, not from the aura. Charge counting cannot see a dispel; cast estimates cannot see a resist. The icon is marked ? while it estimates and corrects itself when combat ends.
  6. TARGETED BY, HEALERS, MOBS and ALLIES count only the units SITREP can see (nameplates, group, target, focus) and only when the client answers the comparison. Counts of zero are not shown.
  7. Shadow health drifts with absorbs and overheal. It resyncs whenever health is plain again.
  8. The matchup matrix holds doctrine defaults, not measurements. Tune it to your realm.
